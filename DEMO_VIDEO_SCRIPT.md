## 🎙️ Full Script (Scene by Scene)

---

### Scene 1: Introduction & The Core Problem
**Time:** `0:00 – 0:30` (30 seconds)  
**Screen Action:**
* Start with the full platform view on screen showing the 3D map with the employee heatmap dots and the clean left panel.
* Gently move cursor over the title: **Colliers Commute Intelligence**.

**Spoken Script:**
> *"Hello Ms. Vijaya! Today, I’m excited to walk you through the **Colliers Commute Intelligence Platform**—a tool built specifically to help corporate leaders make multi-million dollar office relocation and site selection decisions with complete data confidence.*
>
> *Traditionally, companies pick new offices based on gut feeling or simple straight-line distances. But in cities like Mumbai, Bengaluru, and Chennai, straight lines don't show the real picture—bridges, rail choke points, and traffic bottlenecks can make a short distance take over an hour. This platform solves that by analyzing our actual employee locations against real-world transit networks."*

---

### Scene 2: Multi-City Coverage & Property Inspection
**Time:** `0:30 – 1:05` (35 seconds)  
**Screen Action:**
* Click the city dropdown in the top navbar: switch from **Bengaluru** to **Mumbai** (show the cinematic camera glide across the map).
* Hover over the building pin for **One Lodha Place** in Lower Parel—show the hover preview card popping up with building photo, rent (₹245/sq.ft), vacancy, and specs.
* Hover over **Maker Maxity** in BKC.

**Spoken Script:**
> *"Here in the top bar, we can seamlessly switch between Bengaluru, Mumbai, and Chennai. Notice how the map smoothly glides across the city with zero lag.*
>
> *For every city, we have pre-analyzed five benchmark Grade-A commercial properties. When you hover over any property—like **One Lodha Place** here in Lower Parel—you instantly see an executive preview: high-res building images, quoted rental rates, current vacancy, floor plate efficiency, and metro access. Everything you need to evaluate the asset is right at your fingertips without opening endless PDFs."*

---

### Scene 3: Turn-by-Turn Commute Corridors
**Time:** `1:05 – 1:45` (40 seconds)  
**Screen Action:**
* Click on **One Lodha Place** on the map (or in the left sidebar).
* Watch as all 500 employee routes instantly illuminate on the map in curving, glowing color-coded corridors.
* Zoom in slightly onto South Mumbai and the Eastern Freeway / Sea Link to show the paths following the actual curves of roads and bridges.

**Spoken Script:**
> *"Now, watch what happens when I click on One Lodha Place.*
>
> *Instantly, the platform traces the exact daily commute paths for all 500 of our employees. These are not straight lines cutting across the water—every single route is powered by high-resolution turn-by-turn routing with over 370 GPS points per path.*
>
> *You can see employees coming across the Bandra-Worli Sea Link from the Western Suburbs, and via the Eastern Freeway from Navi Mumbai. Green and blue lines show quick, comfortable commutes, while orange and red highlight routes over 45 minutes. This immediately shows you which departments or teams would face transit friction if we move here."*

---

### Scene 4: Commute Catchments & Travel Isochrones
**Time:** `1:45 – 2:15` (30 seconds)  
**Screen Action:**
* In the top navbar, click on the **Commute Catchments** tab.
* Show the left sidebar update with isochrone bands (15, 30, 45, 60 minutes) and the multimodal split breakdown.
* Point to the colored travel-time zones rendered over the map.

**Spoken Script:**
> *"Next, let’s switch to the **Commute Catchments** view.*
>
> *This shows us travel-time isochrones—the colored contours show exactly how far someone can travel in 15, 30, 45, or 60 minutes from this building. The left panel breaks down our workforce by transit mode—whether they take the Metro, suburban trains, two-wheelers, or cabs.*
>
> *For Ms. Vijaya, this answers the most critical HR question: 'What percentage of our workforce stays within the 45-minute comfort zone?' If that number drops below 75%, we know attrition risk spikes."*

---

### Scene 5: Executive Analytics Dashboard & Scorecards
**Time:** `2:15 – 2:45` (30 seconds)  
**Screen Action:**
* Click the **Analytics Dashboard** button in the top navbar.
* Scroll through the modal: highlight the side-by-side comparison table between the 5 properties.
* Point out: **Average Commute Time**, **Workforce Retention Risk**, and **Annual Scope 3 CO₂ Emissions**.
* Close modal and briefly click the **Workforce Database** tab to show the full 1,500 employee searchable table.

**Spoken Script:**
> *"To present these findings to the board, you can open the **Executive Analytics Dashboard**.*
>
> *Here, all candidate properties are ranked side-by-side on an objective scorecard. You can compare the average commute time, monthly travel expenses, retention risk, and even corporate Scope 3 carbon emissions. For instance, choosing an office near a metro hub reduces our annual footprint by over 40 metric tons of CO₂.*
>
> *And if you ever need to inspect individual records, the **Workforce Database** tab gives you a complete, searchable breakdown of all 1,500 employees."*

**Time:** `2:45 – 3:00` (15 seconds)  
**Screen Action:**
* Click the **Tour Guide** button in the top navbar to show the interactive walkthrough card appear in the center-map area without blurring the sidebar.

**Spoken Script:**

>
> *Ms. Vijaya, with this platform, our site selection is 100% data-backed, employee-centric, and boardroom-ready. I’d love to explore your thoughts and run any custom scenarios you have in mind. Thank you!"*

## 💡 Quick Tips for Haroon While Recording
1. **Pacing:** Speak at a calm, conversational speed (~130 words per minute). Don't rush through the numbers—let them land.
2. **Cursor Discipline:** Avoid wiggling the mouse. Hover purposefully on the exact button or metric you are talking about.
3. **Key Highlight Words:** Emphasize *"real-world transit"*, *"turn-by-turn"*, *"comfort zone"*, and *"data-backed"*.
4. **Resolution:** Record your screen at 1080p (1920x1080) for maximum clarity on the 3D map lines and sidebar numbers.
