## 🎙️ Full Script (Scene by Scene)

---

### Scene 1: Introduction & The Chennai CRE Problem
**Time:** `0:00 – 0:30` (30 seconds)  
**Screen Action:**
* Start with the full platform view on screen showing the Chennai 3D map with the employee heatmap dots across Guindy, OMR, Anna Nagar, and Tambaram.
* Gently move cursor over the title: **Colliers Commute Intelligence**.

**Spoken Script:**
> *"Hello Ms. Vijaya! Today, I’m excited to walk you through the **Colliers Commute Intelligence Platform**—a tool built specifically to help corporate leaders evaluate multi-million dollar office relocation and site selection decisions in Chennai with complete data confidence.*
>
> *Traditionally, companies evaluate Chennai offices using simple straight-line distances or developer brochures. But in Chennai, straight lines don't show the real picture—whether it's the Kathipara junction bottleneck, GST Road traffic, or OMR toll plazas, an office that looks close on paper can easily result in an exhausting 75-minute daily commute. This platform solves that by analyzing our actual employee locations against Chennai’s real-world transit network."*

---

### Scene 2: Chennai Micro-Markets & Property Inspection
**Time:** `0:30 – 1:05` (35 seconds)  
**Screen Action:**
* Point to the candidate offices in the left sidebar: Guindy, OMR (Perungudi & Thoraipakkam), Porur (Mount-Poonamallee), and Taramani.
* Hover over the building pin for **Olympia Tech Park** in Guindy—show the hover preview card popping up with building photo, rent (₹95/sq.ft), vacancy, and specs.
* Briefly hover over **DLF Cybercity** in Porur and **RMZ Millenia** on OMR.

**Spoken Script:**
> *"For Chennai, we have benchmarked five premier Grade-A tech parks across the city’s primary commercial micro-markets: Guindy Central, the OMR Tech Corridor, and the Mount-Poonamallee West Corridor.*
>
> *When you hover over any property—like **Olympia Tech Park** here in Guindy—you instantly see an executive preview: high-resolution building photography, quoted base rent of ₹95 per square foot, LEED Platinum rating, and its key differentiator—direct access to the Guindy Metro interchange just 200 meters away.*
>
> *We can instantly contrast this with **DLF Cybercity** in Porur at ₹75 per square foot or **RMZ Millenia** on OMR, giving you complete clarity on asset specifications at a single glance."*

### Scene 3: Turn-by-Turn Commute Corridors
**Time:** `1:05 – 1:45` (40 seconds)  
**Screen Action:**
* Click on **Olympia Tech Park** on the map (or in the left sidebar).
* Watch as all 500 Chennai employee commute paths instantly illuminate across the map in glowing, color-coded corridors.
* Zoom in slightly toward Guindy and Kathipara to show the paths following the actual curves of Anna Salai, GST Road, and the Inner Ring Road.

**Spoken Script:**
> *"Now, watch what happens when I select Olympia Tech Park.*
>
> *Instantly, the platform traces the exact daily commute paths for all 500 of our Chennai employees. Notice that these are not straight lines cutting across city blocks—every single path is computed using high-resolution turn-by-turn routing with over 370 GPS coordinates.*
>
> *You can see employees streaming down from Anna Nagar along the Inner Ring Road, coming up from Tambaram and Chromepet via the GST Road and suburban rail corridor, and crossing from Velachery. Green and blue lines indicate comfortable commutes under 30 minutes, while amber highlights longer journeys. This immediately shows you that Olympia Tech Park serves as a natural transit funnel for the entire Chennai workforce."*

### Scene 4: Commute Catchments & Travel Isochrones
**Time:** `1:45 – 2:15` (30 seconds)  
**Screen Action:**
* In the top navbar, click on the **Commute Catchments** tab.
* Show the left sidebar update with isochrone bands (15, 30, 45, 60 minutes) and the modal split breakdown.
* Point to the colored travel-time polygons expanding outward across Chennai.

**Spoken Script:**
> *"Next, let’s switch to the **Commute Catchments** view.*
>
> *These multi-band isochrones show exactly how far our workforce can travel from this building in 15, 30, 45, or 60 minutes. The left panel breaks down our team by transit mode—including Chennai Metro Blue Line commuters, suburban train riders, two-wheelers, and cabs.*
>
> *For Ms. Vijaya, this answers the most critical workplace question: **'What percentage of our workforce stays within the 45-minute comfort zone?'** For Olympia Tech Park, an incredible **91% of our employees reach the office in under 45 minutes**, with an average transit time of just 24 minutes. If we relocated to Porur, that comfort zone drops to 63%—signaling a severe talent retention risk."*

1. **Pronunciation & Landmarks:** Confidently mention Chennai landmarks: *Guindy Metro*, *Kathipara*, *GST Road*, *OMR*, *Anna Nagar*, and *Tambaram*. This immediately establishes local real estate mastery.
2. **Pacing:** Speak at a calm, conversational speed (~130 words per minute). Pause for 1–2 seconds after clicking Olympia Tech Park to let the glowing transit lines illuminate before speaking.
3. **Key Highlight Numbers:** Emphasize *"91% within 45 minutes"* and *"24-minute average commute"* for Olympia Tech Park compared to Porur.
4. **Resolution:** Record your screen at 1080p (1920x1080) in full-screen (`F11`) with browser zoom at 100%.
